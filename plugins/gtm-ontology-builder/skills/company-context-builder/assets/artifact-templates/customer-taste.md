## Content triggers

Record hooks, stories, proof, and formats that earned attention in the scoped
audience. Buying proof alone does not establish a content trigger. Unsupported
predictions remain inferred hypotheses.

## Anti-triggers

Record specific phrases, tones, formats, or claims that caused dismissal or loss
of trust, with observation scope and evidence. Distinguish this from purchasing
blockers and factual claim restrictions.

## Situational framings

Select recognizable scenes from the canonical workflow and struggling moment.
Label constructed scenes as illustrative; do not present invented scenes as interviews.

## Trusted voices

Describe what lends credibility to a source or author and the observed basis.
Use role or source archetypes where possible; do not assume followers imply trust.

## Review and learning

Record what actual audience response changed, which hypotheses remain untested,
and the next evidence needed. Buyer Sim feedback stays inferred and cannot confirm
audience behavior. Real observations may become learned after review; keep unknowns
explicit and do not create a complete profile from unsupported assumptions.
