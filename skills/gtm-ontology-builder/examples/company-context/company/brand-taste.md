# Acme Analytics brand taste

This is synthetic editorial context for the Acme example. Convictions and copy
below illustrate company choices; they are not evidence from a real founder or
customer. Company strategy remains the source of mission and portfolio decisions.

## Point of view

| Belief | Canonical basis | Expression implication |
|---|---|---|
| A useful data workflow needs an owner and explainable definitions | Company strategy's governance and traceability choices | Lead with the owned work and show how a number or delivery can be checked |
| More dashboards are not automatically progress | Company strategy's preference for defined commerce workflows | Show the decision or recurring activity before introducing the interface |
| Adoption is shared work | Company strategy's bounded onboarding and customer responsibilities | Name customer effort and product boundaries; avoid effortless-transformation language |

These are synthetic strategic preferences, not researched universal market claims.

## Voice and language

- Use direct, practical language: name the workflow, actor, mechanism, and limit.
- Prefer a concrete review or delivery example to abstract promises of better decisions.
- Explain governance in terms of definitions, access, owners, and recovery.
- Use calm confidence and acknowledge what remains unknown; avoid invented urgency.
- Keep terminology appropriate to the role. Technical specificity may increase for
  Data Activation while the shared standard of explainability stays the same.

## Editorial judgment

A draft earns publication when the reader can recognize the work, understand the
mechanism, and see what evidence or next step would resolve their question. Reject
polished sentences that leave the workflow unnamed. Humor must not trivialize
implementation, consent, or operational responsibility. A strong hook still needs
product-specific proof; taste never authorizes a stronger promise.

## Accepted and rejected examples

All copy below is illustrative, not customer language or published performance data.

| Context | Accepted direction | Rejected direction | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Educational commerce article | Before the weekly review, agree which definition each team is using. | Unlock the power of your data with revolutionary analytics. | The first gives a bounded activity; the second provides no mechanism |
| Activation guide | Decide who responds when a delivery fails before the first production run. | Set it and forget it: your warehouse now drives growth. | Shared operational responsibility is explicit rather than hidden |
| Product description | Show the supported scope, the workflow, and the relevant evidence. | One platform that solves every commerce data problem. | Portfolio and product boundaries constrain the claim |

## Narrative principles

Start with the customer's work and current alternative, then introduce the
available mechanism and the evidence needed to assess it. Give the customer agency:
software supports a workflow; people own the commercial decisions and adoption.

Brand Story derives from company strategy. Product Story derives from approved
product truth and positioning for the selected group. Thought Leadership Story
expresses the convictions above without inventing an origin story. Customer Story
requires actual customer evidence; none exists outside the synthetic scenario.
These story forms are outputs, not additional canonical strategy artifacts.
