# Taste discovery and review

Read this reference when audience reception or brand expression is in scope.
Use the artifact guide and optional templates for the durable output contracts.

## Scope

Agree which product groups and canonical buying/adoption roles need Customer Taste,
which channels or awareness stages matter, and who owns Brand Taste. Keep one
customer file per group initially, with scoped observations inside it. Split only
when material differences or size make selective reading useful. Both artifact
types are optional; a missing profile does not block qualification or other work.

## Sources and extraction

Start with supplied materials: interviews, calls, support conversations, approved
content, editorial decisions, and actual content responses. For each source, extract
communication reception separately from purchase behavior:

- the starting attitude and what the evidence actually shows;
- exact non-identifying language, clearly separated from paraphrases;
- which story, example, proof, or format earned attention or changed understanding;
- where wording, tone, or format caused dismissal or loss of trust;
- recurring scenes grounded in the canonical workflow;
- characteristics of voices or sources the audience trusts, and why.

Tag each observation with role, situation, awareness stage, channel when relevant,
safe evidence description, provenance, status, confidence, verification date or
cadence, and sample limitations. A transcript can support observed language and
reactions during that conversation; it cannot alone prove what stops a feed scroll.
Review won, lost/no-decision, support, and content responses when authorized. Do not
expand CRM access or the won cohort silently. Report source and selection bias.

External reviews and communities may fill gaps after source scope is agreed.
Establish audience relevance; a popular post or public reviewer is not automatically
representative of the selected role. Unsupported trigger and emotional predictions
remain inferred. Store public-safe patterns, not raw transcripts or identifying
quotations. Never place a paraphrase or constructed scene in quotation marks as
customer evidence.

For Brand Taste, collect convictions and their real basis from the brand owner,
then review actual accepted/rejected examples and the reason for each decision.
Do not invent founder experiences, contrarian beliefs, or customer endorsement.
Link shared choices to strategy and factual promises to approved product/positioning
sources. Preferences are declared choices; audience effects need their own evidence.

## Confirmation and learning

Show scoped observations and proposed editorial rules for review. Confirmation
changes status, not source. A reviewed hypothesis remains inferred; learned requires
reviewed real observations with evidence. Use claim_refs for consequential or
changing hypotheses needing separate review, without registering every phrase.

Buyer Sim is an optional drafting aid. Its feedback can suggest a revision or an
experiment, but cannot establish purchase intent, certify content triggers, or turn
a simulated reaction into real audience evidence. A brand-aware sim also knows the
seller's desired story; account for that bias when interpreting its praise.

Review Customer Taste at a suggested 90d cadence and Brand Taste at 180d, sooner
after material evidence or owner decisions. Campaign data remains in its source or
temporary workspace; only reviewed durable conclusions enter company context.

## Content review

Generate equivalent drafts from the same brief, product facts, and model, with and
without the taste artifacts. Ask reviewers to compare drafts without seeing which
condition produced them. Check specific audience language, recognizable situations,
brand choices, product/claim correctness, and editing effort. Keep this evaluation
separate from deterministic routing/provenance traces. Validate promising changes
against actual audience behavior; production speed and sim ratings alone do not
establish resonance.
